Last updated: October 7, 2026

Welcome to Zenvyra AI. We are committed to preserving the confidentiality, integrity, and responsible handling of information entrusted to our educational platform. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, processed, retained, and protected when you access or use Zenvyra AI.

Depending on how you interact with Zenvyra AI, we may process information voluntarily provided by you, including account details, authentication information, profile information, and other information supplied during registration or use of the platform.

When you interact with our educational tools, the content you voluntarily submit may also be processed. This may include questions, conversations, prompts, assignments, documents, photographs, and other educational material.

Information processed through Zenvyra AI may be utilized for legitimate operational and service-related purposes, including:

  • Providing educational and artificial-intelligence functionality.
  • Processing questions, prompts, images, and uploaded files.
  • Maintaining authentication, preferences, and conversation features.
  • Monitoring applicable usage limitations and preventing misuse.
  • Maintaining platform security, stability, and reliability.
  • Responding to support requests and service-related inquiries.
  • Complying with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

Zenvyra AI employs artificial-intelligence technologies to generate educational explanations, study assistance, assessments, teaching resources, and other related content.

Information submitted through AI-powered features may be transmitted to the infrastructure and service providers required to process the request and return an appropriate response.

Users should refrain from submitting confidential, highly sensitive, or unnecessary personal information within prompts, conversations, documents, or images.

When you voluntarily upload a document, photograph, worksheet, assignment, or other file, that material may be processed for the specific functionality requested through the platform.

We implement reasonable technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, misuse, or destruction.

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide requested functionality, maintain platform security, prevent abuse, resolve disputes, and satisfy applicable legal obligations.
